How to Use a YouTube Shorts Link with SnapYT

A step-by-step explanation of using public YouTube Shorts links with SnapYT.

YouTube Shorts links can look different from standard video links. A Shorts URL commonly contains the word shorts followed by the video identifier. SnapYT accepts supported public Shorts links as well as standard YouTube watch links and youtu.be share links.

Copy the Shorts link

Open the Short in YouTube, choose Share, and copy the link. Paste the complete link into SnapYT's URL field on the home page. Select Get Details and wait for the public metadata request to finish.

What information can appear?

For a public Short that is available through the YouTube Data API, SnapYT may show its title, channel name, description and thumbnail options. The returned fields depend on what YouTube makes available for that video.

If the link fails

Make sure you copied the full link and that the Short is public. A removed, private, age-restricted or otherwise restricted video may not return details. You can also try opening the link in a browser to check that it is still available.

What SnapYT does not do

SnapYT does not save or download the Short's video or audio. It only works with public metadata and thumbnail images provided by YouTube. Respect the creator's rights when reusing any image or text.
