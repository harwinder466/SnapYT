About SnapYT

SnapYT is a free-to-use web utility designed to make it easier to work with publicly available YouTube video information. Paste a public video or Shorts link to view the thumbnail sizes that are actually available, copy the complete title, or copy the full description.

Our focus is a small set of clear tools that do one job: help creators, editors and viewers access public video metadata without clutter. SnapYT does not download YouTube video or audio streams, does not bypass access controls, and cannot retrieve details from private, deleted or restricted videos.

SnapYT is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube names and marks belong to their respective owners.
