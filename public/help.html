Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about using SnapYT's public video metadata tools.

How do I use SnapYT?

Copy a public YouTube video or Shorts URL, paste it into the box on the home page and select Get Details. When details load, use the buttons to copy the title or full description, or choose an available thumbnail size to download.

Why does a video link not work?

Check that the URL belongs to YouTube and that the video is public and available. Private, deleted, age-restricted or region-restricted videos may not return metadata. Try copying the link again from the video's Share option.

Why are some thumbnail sizes missing?

Not every video has every thumbnail resolution. SnapYT only displays sizes that are actually available and pass its image checks. It does not create fake 2K or 4K versions of smaller images.

Can I download the full YouTube video?

No. SnapYT does not download YouTube video or audio streams. It only provides public metadata, thumbnail images where available, and copy tools for title and description.

Why is the description empty?

The creator may not have added a description, or YouTube may not return details for that video. Check that the video is public and try again.

Is SnapYT affiliated with YouTube?

No. SnapYT is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
