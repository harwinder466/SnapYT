Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember settings and help a website work.

SnapYT currently uses browser storage to remember your cookie choice (for example, whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies). This preference storage is used to remember your selection and is not an advertising profile. Essential technical storage may be required for basic site functions.

If we add analytics or advertising tools, they may set cookies or use similar technologies to understand site usage, measure ad performance, prevent invalid activity or provide ads. Such optional tools should only be enabled in accordance with applicable law and the consent choices presented on the site. Third-party providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

You can change your optional-cookie choice at any time by selecting Cookie Settings in the footer. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser. Some site preferences may not be remembered if browser storage is disabled.
