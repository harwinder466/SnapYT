Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information SnapYT may process when you visit and use the website. We aim to collect only what is needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

When you submit a public YouTube URL, the video ID is sent to our server and used to request public video metadata from the YouTube Data API. The result includes information such as the title, description, channel name and thumbnail metadata. SnapYT does not ask you to sign in to YouTube and does not request your Google password.

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and error logs for hosting, security and troubleshooting. We do not intentionally ask you to submit sensitive personal information. Do not submit passwords, private links or confidential material.

We may use essential browser storage to remember your cookie/privacy choices. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, prevent fraud, personalize or serve ads, subject to applicable consent and settings. No third-party analytics or advertising tags should be considered active merely because this policy describes a possible future use.

You can manage non-essential cookie choices using the Cookie Settings control on this site. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect the saving of your preferences.

SnapYT does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by our hosting, infrastructure or API providers only as needed to provide and secure the service, or where disclosure is required by law. Data retention depends on provider logs and operational requirements.
