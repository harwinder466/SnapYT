How it works

Paste a public YouTube link and tap Get Details. SnapYT shows the title, full description and available thumbnail sizes. Choose a thumbnail to download, or copy the title or description.

SnapYT does not download YouTube video or audio. It works with public metadata and available thumbnail images only.
